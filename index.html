About VERDANT

Where timeless design meets modern luxury

Our Story

VERDANT was born from a simple belief: that luxury should feel personal, effortless and enduring. Rooted in Karachi, we curate premium fashion for individuals who value quality, craftsmanship and quiet confidence.

Every piece in our collection is selected with intent — from the finest fabrics to the finishing details. We believe in fashion that lasts beyond seasons, pieces that tell a story and become part of yours.

Our Mission

To bring world-class luxury fashion to Pakistan, offering authentic premium products with exceptional service and a seamless shopping experience.

Our Vision

To become Pakistan's most trusted luxury fashion destination — a brand synonymous with elegance, quality and customer obsession.

Quality Commitment

We source only authentic products from trusted partners. Each item is inspected for quality before it reaches you. Your satisfaction is our standard.

Customer Satisfaction

From personalised styling advice to easy returns, we are committed to making every interaction with VERDANT feel effortless and premium.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when placing orders, creating accounts or contacting us — including your name, phone number, email address and delivery address.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to process orders, deliver products, provide customer support and improve our services. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information. In this demo version, data is stored locally in your browser. A production system requires secure server-side storage and encryption.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

This website uses browser localStorage to remember your cart and wishlist. No tracking cookies are used in this demo.

5. Your Rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at hello@verdant.pk.

For privacy questions, contact us at 0316 8920170 or hello@verdant.pk.
